# Project Lantern — fictional SPA extract for testing only
1. Definitions. Completion means completion under clause 5. Business Day means a day other than Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday in England. No actual Completion date is agreed in this fixture.
2. Signing. The Buyer and Seller shall sign the SPA and the Seller shall deliver the agreed disclosure letter at Signing.
3. Conditions. Completion is conditional on the consent of Counterparty A to the change of control under the Supply Agreement. The Seller shall seek that consent before Completion. Schedule 3 identifies the other relevant contracts but is not supplied.
4. Interim. The Seller shall provide the Buyer with the draft funds-flow statement at least five Business Days before Completion.
5. Completion. The Seller shall deliver the share certificates and executed stock transfer forms at Completion. The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at Completion in accordance with the agreed funds-flow statement.
6. Post-Completion. The Seller shall deliver the statutory books no later than three Business Days after Completion. The Buyer shall give the Seller the completion accounts within 60 calendar days after Completion.
7. Warranty. The Seller warrants that no litigation is pending against the Company. This warranty does not itself require a separate closing certificate.
8. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by English law. The target's entity jurisdiction, financing arrangements and regulatory threshold facts are not stated.
