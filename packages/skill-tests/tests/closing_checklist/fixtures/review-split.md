# Project Juniper share purchase agreement extract

Fictional source for review testing. Seller is Harbor Holdings; Buyer is Summit
Investments; Target is Juniper Systems. Signing and Closing are separate.
Business Day means a weekday other than a public holiday in the agreed banking
calendar. The calendar and actual Signing and Closing dates remain unconfirmed.

## 2 Closing conditions

2.1 Closing requires Customer Z's written consent and any investment approval
required by applicable law. Buyer alone may waive Customer Z's consent by signed
notice to Seller. Neither party may waive an applicable legal approval requirement.
No particular investment regime is identified as applicable by this agreement.

2.2 No prohibitory order may be in force at Closing. This requirement cannot be
waived. Closing occurs on the fourth Business Day after satisfaction or permitted
waiver of the conditions in clause 2.1, or another date agreed in writing. Clause
2.2 does not start that four-day period.

2.3 Seller shall use reasonable efforts to obtain Customer Z's consent. Seller
must obtain Buyer's written approval before agreeing an adverse amendment to the
customer contract as part of that process.

## 5 Closing deliveries

5.1 Seller shall procure that its affiliate Harbor Services signs the support
agreement and delivers the signed counterpart to Buyer at Closing. Harbor Services
is not a party to this agreement. Seller shall also deliver to Buyer a certified
copy of Target's approval of that agreement at Closing.

5.2 Buyer and Seller shall approve the deposit instructions and deliver the jointly
approved instructions to the depositary before Buyer transfers the deposit.
If an account changes after approval, Buyer shall independently verify the changed
details and both parties shall approve replacement instructions before transfer.

## 8 Follow-up

8.1 Buyer shall send the acceptance notice to the records custodian once Buyer
receives the migration report. Seller shall procure that the custodian delivers
the indexed archive to Buyer within six calendar days after the custodian receives
that notice. The custodian is not a party to this agreement.

8.2 Seller warrants that Target owns its equipment. There is no separate equipment
certificate delivery under this agreement.

## 12 Termination

12.1 If a clause 2.1 condition remains outstanding at 4 pm on 20 February 2027,
either party may thereafter terminate by written notice, unless its own material
breach caused the failure. This clause states no final deadline for exercising
that right. The parties may agree an extension in writing before termination
takes effect. Neither termination nor extension is automatic.
