# Project Quartz stock purchase agreement extract

Fictional source. Atlas is Seller; Meadow is Buyer; Quartz Inc. is Company.
Execution, exchange and Closing occur in one session. The date is not yet fixed.
No fact in this extract establishes a mandatory statutory filing or approval.

## 3 Exchange

3.1 Seller shall hand Buyer one packet containing the endorsed stock certificates
and the agreed officer certificate at exchange. Both documents share the same
delivery time, recipient and responsible party.

3.2 Buyer shall send the agreed funds before the parties release their signatures.
The signatures must remain held to order until Seller confirms receipt. No
separate interim operating period is contemplated.

## 7 Optional election

7.1 Buyer may elect the optional support service by notice received by Seller
within nine calendar days after Closing. The election expires at the end of that
period. If Buyer elects, Seller must activate the service within two calendar days
after receipt of the election. Buyer has not decided whether to elect.

## 10 Warranty

10.1 Seller warrants that Company has authority to operate its business. This
warranty does not require delivery of an authority certificate or certified minutes.
