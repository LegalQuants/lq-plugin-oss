# Project Kestrel share sale terms

Fictional source. Vendor is Beacon Group; Purchaser is Valley Capital; Company is
Kestrel Devices. Signing and Completion are separate. Working Day uses the parties'
agreed banking calendar, not supplied here. No actual event dates are agreed.

## Article 9 Records

9(a) On receipt of the migration report Purchaser must notify acceptance to the
archive operator. The operator's receipt of that notice starts a six-calendar-day
period for delivery of the indexed archive to Purchaser. Vendor undertakes to
procure that delivery; the operator is not a party to these terms.

## Article 4 Conditions

4(a) A written consent from Customer Q and any legally necessary investment
authorisation are prerequisites to Completion. Only Purchaser may dispense with
the customer consent, by a signed notice to Vendor. Legally necessary authorisation
cannot be dispensed with. These terms do not identify an applicable investment regime.

4(b) Completion is scheduled for the fourth Working Day following fulfilment or
permitted waiver of Article 4(a), unless the parties agree a different date in
writing. Separately, no order prohibiting Completion may subsist at Completion;
that non-waivable requirement does not trigger the four-day calculation.

4(c) Vendor must pursue Customer Q's consent using reasonable efforts, obtaining
Purchaser's written approval before accepting an adverse contract amendment.

## Article 6 Completion mechanics

6(a) Beacon Operations, which is not a party here, is to sign the support agreement
and deliver its signed counterpart to Purchaser at Completion. Vendor is obliged
to procure both acts. A certified copy of the Company's approval of that agreement
must also be delivered by Vendor to Purchaser at Completion.

6(b) Before Purchaser transfers the deposit, both parties must approve instructions
and supply the jointly approved instructions to the depositary. A subsequent
account change requires Purchaser's independent verification and both parties'
replacement approval before transfer.

## Article 11 Rights

11(a) A condition in Article 4(a) still outstanding at 4 pm on 20 February 2027
allows either party to terminate thereafter by written notice, other than a party
whose material breach caused that failure. No expiry of this right is specified.
An extension requires written agreement before termination takes effect. Nothing
here brings about automatic termination or extension.

## Schedule W Assurance

W1 Vendor warrants ownership of Company equipment. No separate equipment
certificate is required to be delivered.
